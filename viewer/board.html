Your ESPN draft list, in ESPN's order — with each player checked against what the rest of the fantasy world pays for him.

How to read this board

ADP means Average Draft Position: across thousands of real drafts, the average pick number where a player gets taken. ADP 34 means he usually goes around the 34th pick of the draft.

Every player here gets two numbers: ESPN (where ESPN drafters take him — your draft room) and Market (where everyone else takes him, from the source you pick below). The colored tag compares them:

WAIT +63
Green = ESPN drafters take him 63 picks LATER than the market. He'll probably still be sitting there long after his market price, so you don't need to spend an early pick — wait and get him cheap.
HURRY −40
Red = ESPN drafters grab him 40 picks EARLIER than the market. If you want him, market price is irrelevant — in your ESPN room he'll be gone. Take him early or let him go.
FAIR
Gray = ESPN and the market land within 3 picks of each other. They agree; no edge either way.

The thin bar under each player is the same number as the tag, drawn as a picture: the center tick is "ESPN agrees with the market", green grows to the right (falls further on ESPN), red grows to the left (goes earlier on ESPN). Longer bar = bigger disagreement, until it maxes out at a 45-pick gap — past that, read the number on the tag.

During your draft: tap a player when he gets taken — he dims and crosses out. "Hide drafted" sweeps them away so you only see who's left; "Clear drafted" un-crosses everyone for your next draft. Your taps are remembered on this device. The "Biggest WAIT" and "Biggest HURRY" sort buttons rank the whole board by those same green and red tags.

Compare ESPN against which market?
ESPN's habits, position by position

The typical gap at each position, in picks, against the market you picked. "Falls 17" with a green bar: ESPN drafters let that whole position slide 17 picks — wait on it. "Reaches 11" with a red bar: they take that position 11 picks early — you'll pay above market. "No lean": ESPN treats it like everyone else.

Where the numbers come from. ESPN: rank and ADP for their top 200, captured 2026-08-26. Consensus: 7,726 real 12-team PPR drafts (FantasyFootballCalculator, Aug 18–25). Underdog: their published ADP as of Aug 25, 12-team half-PPR best-ball — no kickers or defenses, which is why K and DEF rows show no Underdog number.

Small print. Your league is 10-team, and the consensus samples are 12-team. Pick numbers still compare one-to-one, but a "round" is different, so every round shown here is computed for 10 teams. The ESPN numbers were transcribed from a screen recording of the draft room — spot-check anything that looks off before trusting it with a real pick.
